About

Sébastien Tran is the Dean of Audencia Business School (founded 1900, triple-crown accredited: EQUIS, AACSB & AMBA), one of Europe's leading business schools with campuses in Nantes, Paris, La Roche-sur-Yon, Barcelone, Shenzhen, Shanghai, Chengdu and São Paulo. He holds a PhD in Industrial Organization from Paris Dauphine University (EQUIS accredited) and a Habilitation to Supervise Research (HDR) in Management Sciences from the University of Rouen.

With more than 20 years of experience in higher education leadership across multiple institutions, he has published extensively on innovation management, strategic management, and Information Systems. His current research focuses on managerial innovation, the future of work, Artificial Intelligence and Entrepreneurship.

About Audencia

Founded in 1900, Audencia is among the best European business schools. Regularly ranked in the world's upper tier by The Financial Times, Audencia is accredited by EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA. It is therefore part of the small number of schools worldwide who hold this 'triple crown' accreditation. Audencia campuses are based in Nantes, Paris, La Roche-sur-Yon, Barcelone, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Chengdu and São Paulo.

First French management school to adhere to the UN's Global Compact, and a signatory of its Principles of Responsible Management Education, Audencia has long been committed to educating and guiding tomorrow's responsible managers and entrepreneurs. Audencia offers programmes in management and communication from bachelor to doctoral level. The school has signed accords with 280 international academic institutions and more than 180 national and international firms. Audencia is home to more than 8,800 students, close to 180 faculty members and has a network of over 47,000 alumni.
